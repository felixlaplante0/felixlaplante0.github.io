I started my PhD in **causality** in October 2026 at the Laboratoire de Mathématiques et Modélisation d’Évry (LaMME), Université Paris-Saclay, under the supervision of [Christophe Ambroise](https://ambroise.cloud), [Gilles Blanchard](https://www.imo.universite-paris-saclay.fr/~blanchard/) and [Pierre Humbert](https://pierrehmbt.github.io). I completed my master’s in mathematics and artificial intelligence at Université Paris-Saclay, where I graduated at the top of my class. I’m interested in causal discovery, optimal transport, conformal prediction and statistical fairness. I enjoy working on the theory as much as developing algorithms and making them run faster in Python.

I play the piano regularly, enjoy gardening and travel whenever I get the chance. I have a strange fascination with the Alps (I’m especially fond of the Swiss Alps), where the quiet vastness of the mountains reminds me how big the world is and how much joy I find in exploring it.

I also do macro photography. I like taking the time to look closely at insects, spiders and small plants that usually go unnoticed, and trying to do them justice in a photograph.
