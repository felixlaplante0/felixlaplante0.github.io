---
title: Uniform High-Probability Bands for Survival Functions
kind: Poster
event: StatMathAppli
place: Fréjus
date: 2025-09
---

Presents asymptotic uniform confidence bands for survival estimators derived under a joint modelling framework. The bands hold with high probability uniformly over time, providing rigorous inferential guarantees for censored survival data beyond classical pointwise confidence intervals.
