---
title: Selecting the Number of Clusters in a Clustering Algorithm
kind: Talk
event: Journées de la Statistique (SFdS)
place: Marseille
date: 2025-06
---

Introduces a data-driven model selection criterion for choosing the number of classes in the Spectral Bridges clustering algorithm. The method leverages the scale-invariant affinity structure of the algorithm to define a connectivity-based score, avoiding cross-validation and working directly from the spectral decomposition.
